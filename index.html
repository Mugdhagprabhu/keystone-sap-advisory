About

I build the piece that holds it together.

I am an SAP Solution Architect specialising in Project System. Over the past 22 years I have worked across construction, manufacturing, chemicals, pharma, telecom, hospitality, media, and power and utilities.

Most of my work is connecting Project System to the rest of the business: Finance, Controlling, Procurement, Sales, Plant Maintenance, and Logistics. The aim is one system your teams can actually rely on. I run the executive workshops, do the fit-gap analysis, design the blueprint, and stay through build, testing, cutover, go-live, and hypercare. I do not hand the hard part to someone else.

So far that has meant seven end-to-end S/4HANA implementations, five on ECC, three global rollouts, and one Commercial Project Management delivery. I did that work at TCS, Infosys, PwC, and Capgemini and EY. Now I take it on directly, through Keystone SAP Advisory.
