Brand Guide

Keystone SAP Advisory

The visual system for Ganesh Prabhu's independent SAP consulting practice. A keystone is the stone that locks an arch together. The mark, the name, and the tone all point to that idea: the piece that makes a transformation hold.

01 · Logo

The keystone mark

A keystone stone with a Space Grotesk K set inside it. Use the two-tone navy stone on light backgrounds and the accent-blue stone on dark. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the K. Do not stretch it, recolour it outside the palette, or add effects.

04 · Voice & Tone

How Keystone speaks

  • Senior and plain-spoken. Confidence comes from clarity, not jargon. Say what was delivered and what it achieved.
  • First person, accountable. “I design… I stay accountable for the outcome.” The practice is one trusted expert, not a faceless firm.
  • Specific over grand. Real numbers (22+ years, 7 S/4HANA implementations) and real scope (blueprint → go-live → hypercare) beat superlatives.
  • Restrained. Generous whitespace, few words, no hype. The design should feel as considered as the work.
